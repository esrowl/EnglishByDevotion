# Advanced Grammar & CEFR assessment — implementation report

EnglishByDevotion · Esra · 18 October 2026

## Delivered

The existing application was extended in place. No rebuild of its architecture,
visual redesign, database reset, new provider, model installation or dependency
was introduced. Writing, Continuous Study selection, vocabulary SRS and external
dictionary integrations were not changed.

- **36 local lessons:** the original 13 plus 23 extensions, primarily B2–C1.
  All six approximate A1–C2 pathways are accessible; C2 has one extension unit.
- **122 objective questions:** all original 65 retained, plus 57 new authored
  questions with explicit keys and explanations. New controlled transformations,
  correction and contextual selection complement the original five exercise types.
- **Layered teaching:** conceptual explanation, form, context, explained examples,
  contrastive understanding, mistakes, practice, summary and later review.
- **Separate skills:** recognition and controlled production summaries; recurring
  task errors; preferred-level, weak-topic and due-review recommendations. The
  practice-focus selector can skip directly to a production task.
- **Optional adaptive diagnostic:** 40-item authored bank, 20 unique questions per
  assessment, beginning near B2, four responses per domain. Domains are grammar,
  vocabulary, reading, contextual usage and controlled sentence construction.
  Actual responses determine approximate overall/domain pathways, relative
  strengths and linked improvement topics. Users can skip, resume, retake, edit
  their level and explore every lesson. Answer explanations appear after completion.
- **Original production:** each lesson accepts original responses as ungraded
  local records. The pre-existing optional local-model adapter now supports these
  registered Grammar tasks, separately from objective grading and mastery.
- **Vocabulary connection:** two authored contexts teach deteriorate with perfect
  continuous and mitigate with modal passive. A saved-library bridge exposes their
  lessons when those words exist in the personal vocabulary library. Grammar
  attempts do not change vocabulary familiarity or SRS.
- **Data safety:** additive Dexie version 8, full JSON export/import, transactional
  validation and merge, disk auto-backup, and older-client omission safeguards for
  the three new stores. Existing lesson/question IDs and session lengths stay stable.

The full curriculum list, algorithm, contracts, migration and limits are documented
in [GRAMMAR_LEARNING.md](../GRAMMAR_LEARNING.md).

## Storage and reliability

Existing `grammarProgress` and `grammarAttempts` remain authoritative for objective
lessons, sessions and delayed retention. New stores are `grammarPlacements`,
`grammarPreferences`, `grammarProduction`. All are in the existing `LexoraDB`.
No pre-existing store is cleared or rewritten by the v8 migration.

Recognition is choice-based evidence; typed and ordered objective answers are
controlled production. Original free writing and AI comments cannot confer mastery.
Mastery still requires reading completion and successful full exercise sessions,
including a review at least 24 hours later. Old five-question sessions remain
five-question sessions, so previously established retention is not invalidated.

## Limits and additional setup

- The diagnostic is **uncalibrated**, approximate and low-confidence: four items
  per area cannot establish official CEFR proficiency. Item-level labels are
  editorial. Listening, speaking and unrestricted writing are not assessed.
- Controlled transformations use explicit finite keys, not a semantic writing
  evaluator. Some otherwise valid alternatives may be unmatched; the UI states
  this. Original production is deliberately ungraded.
- No local model is configured for real inference here. Only the existing adapter
  contract was fixture-tested. Advisory feedback requires the user’s already
  installed local model and `EBD_OLLAMA_MODEL`; nothing was downloaded or installed.
- Vocabulary integration is two curated contexts, not arbitrary exercise generation
  over the entire saved library. Vocabulary and Grammar progress stay distinct.
- The curriculum provides coverage across the requested major topic families,
  not an exhaustive reference or complete certified A1–C2 programme. C2 breadth,
  more diagnostic items, psychometric calibration and independent expert editorial
  review remain future improvements.
- Offline tests cover an already loaded application. Cold-start offline operation
  of the entire app was not implemented or claimed. Disk backup needs the local server.
- No unrelated API availability work, Writing changes, visual redesign or new
  dependencies were included in this Grammar upgrade.
