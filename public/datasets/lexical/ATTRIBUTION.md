# EnglishByDevotion lexical dataset

Contributors: English Wiktionary and Simple English Wiktionary contributors.
Extraction: Kaikki.org / Wiktextract, Tatu Ylonen and contributors.
Prepared for EnglishByDevotion by Esra, 2026-10-18.

Sources:

- [Kaikki English dictionary](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/)
- [Official raw downloads](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/rawdata.html)
- [Simple English raw JSONL download](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/downloads/simple/simple-extract.jsonl.gz)
- Each entry retains its individual Wiktionary article URL and Kaikki download URL.
- Translation supplements retain their own source URLs rather than assuming the primary English definition's source.

Adaptations: select NGSL headwords; filter unusable/inflection-only and marked rare,
obsolete or offensive senses; normalize parts of speech; preserve English sense
glosses, Turkish translation-table glosses and lexical relationship types; omit
third-party quoted examples and audio files. English and Turkish senses are not
automatically forced into one-to-one mappings. No translation is inferred from
word similarity. No CEFR labels are supplied by this dataset.
